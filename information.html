People, Traditions and Culture

About Iran

Nowruz

Nowruz is the Persian New Year and one of the most important celebrations in Iranian culture. It marks the beginning of spring and the renewal of nature, and it is celebrated on the spring equinox, usually on March 20 or 21. Before Nowruz, families clean their homes, buy new clothes, and prepare for the celebration. One of the most famous traditions is setting the Haft-Seen table, which includes seven symbolic items beginning with the Persian letter "S". During Nowruz, families visit relatives and friends, share special meals, exchange gifts, and celebrate new beginnings. Nowruz has been celebrated for thousands of years and is also observed in several other countries.

Iranian Food

Iranian cuisine is very diverse, with a wide variety of traditional dishes, flavours, and ingredients. Each region of Iran has its own special foods, influenced by its local culture, climate, and traditions. Popular dishes include Ghormeh Sabzi, Fesenjan, Dizi, and different types of kebab. Iranian food also includes delicious rice dishes, fresh herbs, soups, breads, and sweets. Ingredients such as saffron, dried lime, pomegranates, and walnuts give many Iranian dishes their unique flavours. Food is an important part of Iranian culture and is often shared with family and friends during gatherings and celebrations.

Art and Crafts

Iranian art has a long and rich history, reflecting the country's culture, creativity, and traditions. Persian carpets are among the most famous examples of Iranian art, known for their detailed patterns and beautiful colours. Other traditional arts include miniature painting, calligraphy, pottery, tilework, and metalwork. Iranian architecture is also famous for its colourful tiles, elegant domes, and detailed decorations. Many of these art forms have been passed down through generations and remain an important part of Iran's cultural heritage today.

Language and Traditions

Persian, also known as Farsi, is the official language of Iran and an important part of its cultural identity. It has a long literary history, with famous poets such as Ferdowsi, Rumi, Hafez, and Saadi. Persian poetry is admired for its beautiful language, deep meanings, and themes of love, nature, and wisdom. Persian is spoken in different regions of Iran, and it has a variety of accents and local dialects. The way people speak can differ in pronunciation, expressions, and some words from one region to another. These differences reflect Iran's cultural diversity and make the language even more interesting. Persian is written from right to left using a script based on the Arabic alphabet, with additional letters. Today, the Persian language continues to connect people with Iran's history, literature, and traditions.
